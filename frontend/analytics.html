    Session-Level Insights

    Select a session to see its own analytics. Ended sessions have a stored final summary.

    How metrics are computed: accuracy scores a Correct reply as 1, Partial as 0.5 and Incorrect as 0, averaged over rated replies. "Fully correct" is the share of rated replies marked Correct. Each reply's position in its session, from first to last, places it in the Start, Middle or End phase. Topics are classified into five AI domains, plus General for anything outside them.
